About the Academy

Developing Champions
On & Off the Pitch

Rising Sport Academy is Kigali's premier youth football development center, nurturing talent from grassroots to elite level since 2021. We provide structured training programs for players aged 6 to 17, guided by experienced coaches committed to excellence both on and off the pitch.
